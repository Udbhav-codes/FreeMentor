How it's built

Soft minimal + light neumorphism

Soft shadows only on cards, the progress ring and checkboxes. Main buttons are solid navy so they always read as tappable.

Type

Your roadmap is ready

Headings: DM Sans (italic for warm moments). UI: Inter. Hindi: Noto Sans Devanagari. Shown here in Inter Display.

Colour roles

Navy: brand, buttons, progress Amber: streak and "today" only Green: completed / good news Soft base: every screen

Roadmap · Progress · Reports · Practice · Profile. The current tab is navy and bold.
