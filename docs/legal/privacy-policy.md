This policy explains what FreeMentor collects, why, how long we keep it and
what you can do about it. It is written to meet the **Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

FreeMentor is operated by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, [REGISTERED ADDRESS]
("we", "us"). Under the DPDP Act we are the **Data Fiduciary** for your
personal data and you are the **Data Principal**.

---

## 1. The short version

- You must be **16 or older** to use FreeMentor. If you are 16 or 17, we need
  your parent or guardian's verified agreement first.
- We collect the minimum needed to build and track your study plan.
- **We never sell your data**, and we never share it for anyone else's
  advertising.
- Your roadmap and progress live on your phone. The server holds a copy so you
  do not lose it when you change device.
- You can export or delete everything, from inside the app or from our website.

---

## 2. Who can use FreeMentor

FreeMentor is for learners aged **16 and above**. We ask for your birth year
during sign-up and refuse accounts below 16. This is enforced both in the app
and in our database, so it cannot be bypassed by a modified app.

**If you are 16 or 17**, the DPDP Act requires *verifiable* consent from your
parent or guardian before we process your data. We send them a one-time code
by email or SMS; entering that code is their consent. A tick-box saying you
have permission is **not** sufficient and we do not treat it as such.

While you are under 18 you will only ever be shown **non-personalised**
advertising rated **G**, and we do not profile you or track your behaviour for
advertising.

## 3. What we collect, and why

### 3.1 When you sign in

We use **Google Sign-In**. From Google we receive only:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Name | To greet you and label your account |
| Email address | To identify your account and contact you about it |

We do **not** receive your Google password, contacts, files, calendar or
anything else. We never see your Google password.

### 3.2 When you set up your plan

| Data | Why | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Birth year | To enforce the 16+ rule and the under-18 advertising rules | Yes |
| Preferred name | Shown in the app | Optional |
| Profile photo | Shown in the app | Optional |
| Qualification / current level | To word the plan and pace it sensibly | Optional |
| Language (English / Hindi / Hinglish) | To pick your resources and app language | Yes |
| Chosen goal and target date | To build the roadmap | Yes |
| Weekly study hours | To schedule your days | Yes |
| Parent or guardian's email or phone | Only if you are 16 or 17, to obtain consent | Conditional |

### 3.3 As you study

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Which tasks you tick off, and when | To track progress and shift the plan when you miss a day |
| Confidence ratings on topics | To schedule revision |
| Test scores you enter yourself | To show your reports and trigger catch-up work |
| Rest days you take | To protect your streak |
| Study streak | To show it |

We do **not** track which videos you watch, how long you watch them, or what
you do on YouTube or any other site. When you open a resource we hand you over
to that site and stop seeing what happens.

### 3.4 When you buy a plan

Payment is handled entirely by **Google Play**. We never see or store your
card, UPI or bank details. We receive from Google only a purchase token and
order ID, which we store to prove you own what you paid for.

### 3.5 Technical data

Crash reports and basic usage analytics (which screens are opened, whether the
app crashed) via Firebase. These contain a device identifier, not your name.
*[Remove this section if Firebase is not in the launched build.]*

## 4. What we do NOT collect

We think the list of things we deliberately do not take is as important as the
list we do:

- No contacts, photos library, files, call logs or SMS
- No precise location — ever
- No background activity, app usage outside FreeMentor, or browsing history
- No behavioural profile for advertising
- No data at all from anyone under 16

## 5. Where your data lives

Your personal data is stored on **Supabase** infrastructure hosted on Amazon
Web Services in the **Mumbai region (ap-south-1), India**. Choosing an Indian
region was deliberate: your data stays in India under normal operation.

Some of our processors (listed below) may process limited data outside India.
The DPDP Act permits transfers except to countries the Central Government
restricts; if that list changes in a way that affects us, we will change
processors or tell you.

## 6. Who else sees your data

We share personal data only with the processors needed to run the service.
**We never sell your data and never share it for third-party advertising.**

| Processor | What they get | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Google (Sign-In)** | Your sign-in | To authenticate you |
| **Google Play Billing** | Your purchase | To take payment and verify it |
| **Supabase** | Your account, plan and progress | Hosting and database |
| **Google AdMob** | Ad request and consent status | To show ads *(free and Basic plans only)* |
| **Firebase** | Crash and usage data | To fix crashes |

Each is bound by its own terms to process data only on our instructions.

We will also disclose data where we are legally required to — a court order,
or a lawful request from an authority — and to protect our rights or someone's
safety.

## 7. Advertising

**YouTube videos in FreeMentor are never put behind an advert or a paywall**,
on any plan. That is a rule we hold ourselves to and a condition of using
YouTube's services.

Where ads appear:

- **Free trial and Basic:** you may be offered a short rewarded ad to open
  certain notes or test links. It is always your choice and never interrupts a
  video, a test or your daily checklist.
- **Premium:** no ads at all.
- **Under 18:** non-personalised ads only, maximum content rating **G**,
  tagged for users under the age of consent.

Before any personalised advertising we ask for your consent through Google's
consent tool, and you can change your answer later in the app.

## 8. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account and progress | While your account exists |
| Deleted accounts | Erased within **30 days** of your request |

## 9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

- **Access** — ask what we hold about you
- **Correct** — fix anything wrong, mostly from Profile in the app
- **Erase** — delete your account and data
- **Withdraw consent** — as easily as you gave it
- **Nominate** — name someone to act for you if you die or become incapacitated
- **Complain** — to us first, then to the Data Protection Board of India

**To delete your account:** Profile → Account → Delete my account, or visit
**[WEBSITE]/delete-account** if you no longer have the app installed. You do
not need the app to delete your data.

**To export your data:** Profile → Account → Export my progress.

We answer requests within **30 days**.

## 10. How we protect it

- Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- Row-level security in the database, so one learner's data is unreachable
  from another's account
- Profile photos in a private store, reachable only through short-lived signed
  links
- Parent consent codes stored **hashed**, never in plain text, expiring in 10
  minutes
- All privileged keys held on our server, never inside the app
- Administrative access limited and logged

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify
you and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Act requires.
