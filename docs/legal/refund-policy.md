# FreeMentor — Refund Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

How refunds work, in plain terms. This policy sits alongside Google Play's own
refund rules and your rights under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** —
neither of which this policy takes away.

---

## 1. Try it before you pay

Every roadmap begins with a **3-day free trial**. No card, no payment, nothing
to cancel.

We put the trial first precisely so you can see the real plan — your actual
daily tasks, the real links, the real schedule — before any money changes
hands. If it is not what you want, simply stop.

---

## 2. Google Play handles payment and refunds

All purchases go through **Google Play Billing**, so refunds are issued by
Google rather than by us directly.

### Within 48 hours of purchase

Ask Google directly — it is the fastest route and usually automatic:

1. Open the **Google Play Store**
2. Tap your profile → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Budget & order history**
3. Find the FreeMentor purchase → **Request a refund**

Or use **[play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory)**.

### After 48 hours

Google's automatic window has closed, but write to us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**
with your order ID and what went wrong. We can ask Google to refund you and we
will where section 3 applies.

---

## 3. When we will refund you

We will support a refund request where:

| Situation | Our position |
|---|---|
| **The app does not work** on your device and we cannot fix it | Full refund |
| **You were charged twice** for the same roadmap | Refund the duplicate |
| **You bought the wrong goal** and have not meaningfully started it | Full refund within 7 days |
| **A paid feature is substantially missing** or broken | Full refund |
| **We discontinue the service** | Fair proportion, per Terms §9 |
| **You were charged after asking us to delete your account** | Full refund |

We read these generously. If something has clearly gone wrong, we would rather
refund you than argue.

---

## 4. When we will usually not refund

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| You changed your mind after using the plan for weeks | The trial exists for this |
| You did not get the exam result you wanted | We never promised one (Terms §1) |
| You did not study | The plan was delivered; the work is yours |
| You bought a different goal by choice | Each roadmap is a separate purchase, stated before you buy |
| A third-party link broke | Report it and we will replace it — that is a fix, not a failure of the plan |

None of this overrides your statutory rights. If you believe a refund is due
under the Consumer Protection Act, tell us and we will look again.

---

## 5. What happens to a refunded purchase

When a refund is issued, access bought with it ends and the roadmap returns to
its locked state. **Your progress is not deleted** — your ticks, scores and
streak stay on your phone and on your account, so if you buy again later you
pick up where you left off.

---

## 6. Coupons and discounts

A refund returns **what you actually paid**, not the undiscounted price. A
coupon used on a refunded purchase may or may not become available again,
depending on its terms.

---

## 7. How long it takes

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| We acknowledge your request | Within **24 hours** |
| We decide | Within **15 days** (usually far sooner) |
| Google processes the refund | Typically **2–7 working days** |
| Money reaches your bank | Depends on your bank, usually **5–10 working days** |
