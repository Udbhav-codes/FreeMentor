# FreeMentor — Content & Copyright Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

How we choose what to link to, what we do and do not do with other people's
work, and how to ask us to remove something.

---

## 1. The one-line version

**We link. We do not host.**

FreeMentor contains no copied lessons, no mirrored videos, no re-uploaded
notes and no scraped text. Every resource opens at its original source, in
that source's own app or website, exactly as its creator published it.

---

## 2. What we actually store

For each resource we keep only what a library catalogue would keep:

- the **URL**
- the **title** as published
- the **creator or channel name**
- the **language**
- an **estimate of how long it takes**
- which topic it belongs to and in what order to use it

That is the whole of it. We store no video files, no transcripts, no PDFs, no
copies of anyone's text.

**What we create ourselves** is the sequence: which topic comes before which,
how long to spend, when to revise, when to test. That ordering is our work and
is what a paid plan pays for.

---

## 3. How a resource gets in

Every link must be:

1. **Free to access** — no paywall, no mandatory subscription, no login we
   cannot describe up front. Where a free account is needed, we say so.
2. **Publicly published** by its creator for public viewing.
3. **Relevant and accurate** for the topic it sits under.
4. **Reachable** — checked automatically and re-checked at least monthly.

We prefer official sources — NCERT, OpenStax, official language and framework
documentation — and established educational channels.

---

## 4. YouTube

YouTube videos are opened in the **YouTube app or website**, where the creator
gets their view, their watch time and their ad revenue exactly as if you had
found the video yourself.

We hold ourselves to these rules:

1. **We never download, re-upload, mirror, embed-to-replace, or strip ads
   from** anyone's video. Every view happens on YouTube.
2. **We never charge for the video itself.** Every video we link to is free on
   YouTube and stays free there, for anyone, with or without FreeMentor. What
   a FreeMentor plan pays for is the roadmap — which video to watch, in what
   order, on which day, and what to do after it.
3. **We never claim or imply ownership** of anyone's content.

A paid plan unlocks the roadmap, and the roadmap contains links. If you would
rather not pay, every one of those videos remains free to find and watch on
YouTube directly — nothing we do removes or restricts that.

## 7. Accuracy, and its limits

We check that links work and that resources are relevant. We **cannot**
guarantee that third-party content is accurate, complete or up to date with
the current syllabus — it is not ours and we do not control it.

Examination syllabi change. We review roadmaps against the current syllabus
and update them, but **always check the official syllabus from the examination
body**. If you spot something out of date, tell us.
