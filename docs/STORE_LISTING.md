```
FreeMentor gives you everything to study for your goal in one place: video lessons, notes, practice questions, recall questions and mock tests, arranged into a plan that tells you exactly what to do each day.

Pick a goal, tell it how many hours you can study each week and your target date, and FreeMentor builds your course. Every day opens with a short checklist. Tap a task and the material is right there: the video plays inside the app, and the notes and practice open with one tap.

WHAT YOU GET FOR EVERY TOPIC
• Video lessons from respected teachers on YouTube, playing inside the app, with a main video and alternatives (including Hindi) when you want a different explanation
• Notes from official documentation and trusted study sites
• Practice questions and previous-year questions (PYQs) for exams
• Recall questions to test yourself before you move on
• Topic tests, plus mock tests and revision in the final part of every roadmap
• Real time estimates for each task, so you know how long today will take

All of this material is free at its source. FreeMentor does not host or copy anyone's content. It picks the right lesson, notes and practice for each topic and puts them in the right order, so you stop searching and start studying.

COURSES AVAILABLE NOW
• Entrance exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET UG, CUET UG
• Programming: Python, Java, C, SQL
• Careers: Full Stack, Frontend and Backend Development, Cybersecurity
More goals are on the way and are shown as "Coming soon" in the app.

A PLAN THAT FITS YOUR LIFE
• Start from your current stage: Class 11, Class 12, dropper, college student or working professional
• FreeMentor tells you whether your target date is realistic and when you will finish
• Miss a day and the plan moves forward on its own. Nothing you completed is ever lost
• Rate your confidence after each topic, and weak topics get extra revision

STAY ON TRACK
• A route map of your whole course, week by week
• Streaks with a grace day and protected rest days
• Daily study reminders at the time you choose
• Weekly reports on what you finished and what needs attention

BUILT FOR INDIAN LEARNERS
• Works in English, Hindi and Hinglish
• Your plan and progress work offline once downloaded

PLANS

Each plan is a single payment for lifetime access to one course. There is no subscription.
```
